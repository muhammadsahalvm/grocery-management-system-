Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are SalamMart's standard delivery hours?

We fulfill deliveries from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM every day of the week, including weekends and public holidays.

How does free delivery work?

All grocery orders totaling ₹499 or more automatically qualify for free doorstep delivery. For orders below ₹499, a nominal delivery fee applies depending on your zone.

What is your quality guarantee and return policy?

If any fruit, vegetable, or grocery product does not meet your expectations upon delivery, simply let our delivery partner know or reach out within 24 hours for a prompt refund or replacement.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We support Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI on Delivery, and secure online digital checkout through Debit / Credit Cards and Net Banking.
